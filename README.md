**Neon Palms — Palmera Bay.** An original top-down open-world crime game that
runs in a desktop browser from static files, with no build step and no network
request. Reyes, a getaway driver with a debt, takes six story jobs for fixer
Mara Voss of The Gilded Gull across a six-district city: walk or drive
anywhere, steal any car, run fares in a taxi, hunt 20 hidden packages — and
mind the witnesses, because the Palmera Bay PD answers crime with patrol
cars, roadblocks of its own making, and officers who will hold you down for
the arrest. Wanted stars rise on seen crimes and cool off in the shadows, or
instantly for $300 at the Shine & Go respray. Everything is drawn and sounded
in code: canvas art, WebAudio-synth effects and music, a day/night cycle, a
live minimap, and a save game that remembers your money, jobs and packages.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

To hand the entry in the way the grader does:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Controls: WASD/arrows to walk and drive · E to enter cars and talk · Shift to
sprint · mouse to aim, click (or J) to punch or fire · Q swaps weapon · P
pauses · M mutes.
